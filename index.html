About

I am a backend developer based in Antananarivo. I hold a bachelor's degree in computer science from the Haute École d'Informatique (HEI), Software Ecosystem track, and spent a year as a work-study student at Numer Madagascar, where I automated invoicing and payment management. Since March 2026, I have been on a permanent contract at Aureon Madagascar, building and maintaining the backend of web platforms used by German-speaking clients in Europe, from health coaching to customer support.

Working with production data every day made me want to go further: to understand what data says, not just store it. I earned the Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate and built Heather, a complete analysis of 45 years of climate data in Madagascar. I now want to deepen my skills in data science and artificial intelligence, and use them on problems that matter to my country.
